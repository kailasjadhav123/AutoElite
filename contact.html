FAQ

Common Questions

What documents do I need to rent? +
A valid driver's license (minimum 2 years), a major credit card in the driver's name, and a valid passport or national ID for international clients.
Is there a minimum rental period? +
Our minimum rental is 1 day (24 hours). For exotic and hypercar rentals, a minimum of 2 days is required. Multi-week rentals receive preferential rates.
Can I add a second driver? +
Yes, additional drivers can be added at time of booking or on delivery. All drivers must meet our standard requirements and be present for license verification.
What is your cancellation policy? +
Free cancellation up to 48 hours before your rental start time. Cancellations within 48 hours are subject to a 50% charge. No-shows are charged in full.
Do you offer airport pickup? +
Absolutely. We offer complimentary meet-and-greet at all major airports in our service cities. The vehicle is waiting for you trackside.
