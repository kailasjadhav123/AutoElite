Our Mission

We Don't Rent Cars.
We Curate Experiences.

AutoElite was founded on a simple conviction: the car you drive shapes the entire quality of your journey. Every traffic light becomes a moment. Every open road, an invitation.

Our concierge team hand-selects each vehicle, maintains it to showroom standard, and delivers it to wherever you are. We don't believe in ordinary.

Our Journey

How We Got Here

From a single Porsche in Los Angeles to a worldwide fleet of the world's finest vehicles.

2012
The Beginning

Marcus Webb rents a single Porsche 911 to a film production in Los Angeles. The feedback is overwhelming.

2015
Fleet Expansion

Fleet grows to 25 vehicles across 3 cities. First corporate contracts signed with Fortune 500 clients.

2018
Digital Transformation

Launch of instant digital booking platform. 60% reduction in booking time, 40% growth in client base.

2021
Going Green

Launch of the Electric Collection — performance EVs delivered with complimentary home charging setup.

Today
18 Cities. 150 Vehicles.

AutoElite is the preferred luxury rental partner for discerning clients across North America, Europe, and the Middle East.
